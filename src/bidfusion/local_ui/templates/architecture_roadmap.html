Boundaries that hold in every phase

These are not open questions. They are properties of the current build, enforced by the code and its tests rather than by convention.

  • The interface makes no network call and contacts no external system.
  • No model is called from the interface, and the model gateway is not connected to the pipeline.
  • Freely uploaded files are quarantined and never parsed, extracted, or cleared.
  • Full analysis runs only on the repository's own fixture packages.
  • Preview pages read no uploaded or quarantined content of any kind.
  • Recorded artifacts are refused rather than shown when their integrity check fails.
  • Production customer data is out of scope until a separate decision permits it.

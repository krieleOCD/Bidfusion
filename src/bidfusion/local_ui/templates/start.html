BidFusion — RFP intelligence

Upload your RFP package and turn it into actionable bid intelligence.

BidFusion securely inventories and quarantines authorized test documents. Cleared packages can later be transformed into evidence-based requirements, deadlines, formalities and reviewable bid intelligence. In this build the two lanes are kept strictly apart: the repository's own synthetic packages run the full local pipeline, while a free upload only ever reaches quarantine.
